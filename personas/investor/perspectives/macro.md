# Perspective: Macro

## Focus Areas

- Interest rate cycles and central bank policy (BOJ, Fed, ECB)
- Economic indicators: GDP, CPI, employment, PMI
- Currency movements and their impact on equity markets
- Cross-market correlations (bonds, equities, commodities)
- Geopolitical risk assessment

## Analytical Framework

- Top-down approach: global economy → sector → individual asset
- Evaluate regime (risk-on / risk-off / transition)
- Consider monetary policy lag effects (6-18 months)
- Watch for divergence between economic data and market pricing

## Biases (Intentional)

- Weight liquidity risk higher than consensus
- Assume mean reversion over multi-year horizons
- Be wary of crowded trades driven by narrative
